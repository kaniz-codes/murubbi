## what murubbi is

**murubbi is a credit bureau for on-time delivery,** built for Bangladesh's garment factories and the buyers who order from them.

Bangladesh doesn't lose orders on price. It loses them on trust:

- **Buyers are moving orders to more predictable countries.** Four EU buyers cut their SS27 orders by 15–35% [1]. EU imports from Bangladesh fell about 19%, while Vietnam's barely moved [2].
- **The reason is delivery.** The 2026 gas crisis cut garment output by 25–40% [3]. Wage protests disrupted 529 factories in a single day [4]. Lead times run 60–95 days, against about 32 for China [5].
- **Buyers find out on ship day,** when the only options left are air freight or a penalty: 3–15% of the order for shipping 1–20 days late [6].
- **Nobody can tell a reliable factory from a late one,** so buyers cut the whole country: the good factories along with the bad.

What murubbi does about it:

1. **The factory hears first.** When a shock is coming (gas, power, flood or unrest), the factory hears about it first, in Bangla, weeks before ship day.
2. **The cheapest fix, priced.** A costed plan instead of an air-freight bill. A human approves every action.
3. **A plan, not a surprise.** Within 24 hours, the buyer sees the risk and the factory's plan together.
4. **Proof.** The buyer confirms every delivery promise, and then it's locked. The record becomes a 0–100 trust score that counts how a factory responds, never the shock that hit it.

## our mission

**Make Bangladesh's delivery predictable, and prove it.** Delays will still happen. Surprises don't have to. When every factory warns early and every promise is on the record, buyers can keep the good factories instead of cutting the whole country.

What we promise along the way:

- **The factory hears first.** No factory is exposed to a buyer without a chance to answer.
- **We score the response, not the shock.** Gas cuts, protests and workers never lower a score.
- **No worker data.** Orders and shipments only.
- **A human approves every action.** The AI suggests and drafts; people decide.
- **Bangla first.** Owners get alerts and briefs in Bangla.
- **Data stays in Bangladesh.**
